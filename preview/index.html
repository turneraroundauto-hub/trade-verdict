Preview staging

This is /preview/ — a hidden subpath on the live domain used to stage in-progress UI work before it's wired into any real tier's nav.

Not linked from anywhere in the live app, excluded from the service worker (sw.js), and disallowed in robots.txt. The Play Store app only ever opens the root URL, so nothing under here ever reaches it.

Real preview content lives at /preview/<name>/ — see CLAUDE.md's "UI staging: the /preview/ subpath convention" section for how to add one.
